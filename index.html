Common questions

Start with the question that's on your mind.

You don't need to be ready to move before reaching out. A good conversation can help you understand what to do next—or whether to do anything yet.

Should I buy before I sell?

It depends on your finances, timing, risk tolerance, and the strength of each market. We can map both paths and compare the tradeoffs before you commit.

What should I fix before listing my home?

Usually, the best work is not the most expensive work. I help prioritize the improvements that protect value, improve presentation, or remove buyer objections.

How do I know whether a property is a good investment?

We look beyond the asking price to repairs, financing, holding costs, rents, resale assumptions, and the risks that could change the outcome.

When should I contact an agent?

Earlier than most people think. Planning ahead gives you more options and reduces rushed decisions, even when your move may be months away.
