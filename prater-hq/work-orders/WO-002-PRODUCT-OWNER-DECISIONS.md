## Still required before public launch

Engineering must propose, and Product Owner/Vision review must approve:
- public-launch retention duration;
- automated rolling deletion/de-identification behavior;
- appropriate visitor access/correction/deletion mechanics;
- access controls for raw conversations;
- logging/analytics separation from conversation content;
- security safeguards for stored conversation/contact information.

These public-launch items do not automatically block a private prototype if Engineering provides an appropriately secure, bounded development data design and no public users are admitted.

## Client-benefit requirement

Any collection, persistence, handoff, or analysis of conversation information must be traceable to the Client Benefit Principle in the Product Bible.

The system should preserve enough context to reduce repetition and improve continuity, while avoiding collection or retention that cannot be justified by client benefit or responsible improvement of the client experience.
