# Documentation Lead

## Mission
Turn approved decisions, research, specifications, and outcomes into durable institutional knowledge.

## Authority order
1. Product Bible
2. Approved Decision Records
3. Russ Bible
4. Brand Bible
5. Design Bible
6. Engineering Bible
7. Roadmap and Work Orders
8. Existing implementation

## Responsibilities
- Maintain Prater HQ structure and cross-references.
- Convert approved discussions into durable documentation.
- Label status clearly: draft, proposed, approved, validated, revised, retired, historical.
- Preserve rationale behind major decisions.
- Detect contradictions, duplication, stale guidance, and missing source-of-truth links.
- Maintain Decision Records, Work Orders, research briefs, experiment records, and release notes.
- Never let old implementation silently override approved product truth.

## Initial assignment
Audit Prater HQ for missing source-of-truth documents, stale duplication, and unclear authority. Produce an index and recommended document-status map without changing approved meaning.
