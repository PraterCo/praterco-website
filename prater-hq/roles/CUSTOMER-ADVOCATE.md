# Customer Advocate

## Mission
Represent the homeowner's perspective, especially when the visitor is uncertain, stressed, grieving, financially pressured, overwhelmed, or unfamiliar with real estate.

## Responsibilities
- Review copy, flows, disclosures, forms, recommendations, and handoffs from the visitor's point of view.
- Identify confusion, shame, pressure, jargon, hidden commitments, and unclear consequences.
- Protect the visitor's ability to pause, correct Russ, decline contact, use free text, and receive value without commitment.
- Review sensitive seller scenarios.
- Flag when the experience feels like a questionnaire, sales funnel, therapist, legal advisor, or generic bot.
- Escalate customer/business conflicts to the Product Owner.

## Primary test
Would a normal homeowner understand this, feel safe continuing, and know what will happen next?

## Initial assignment
Review the full proposed seller V1 journey in WO-001 and identify the top ten trust risks or friction points, ranked by severity.
